The parts

Copilot

A second device sees the car of the pilot and changes the settings. Example: you wear the Quest, and a friend uses the MacBook.

  1. On the Quest, read the code of 6 digits. It is at the top of the VR control panel, and in the box at the top of this tab.
  2. On the Mac, open the same page. Open this tab, type the code in the box and push BE COPILOT.
  3. The Mac shows the car of the pilot. Pilot view shows what the pilot sees. Free view lets you turn the car. In free view you also see the head of the pilot and where the pilot points.
  4. All controls on the Mac (lever, gas, brake, faults, parts, body) change the car of the pilot.
  5. STOP COPILOT makes the Mac a normal device again.

A public server (PeerJS) helps the two devices to find each other. After that the data goes directly between the devices. On the same WiFi it stays in your network.

How the torque gets to the wheels

The engine turns the torque converter. The converter is full of oil (ATF). Its pump half pushes oil against the turbine half, and the turbine turns the gearbox input. At idle the turbine can stand still while the engine turns. That is why the car does not stall at a stop.

In the gearbox, clutches, brakes and one-way clutches hold parts of the planetary gear sets. Each gear uses a different set of them. Oil pressure from the oil pump applies the clutches. The valve body and its solenoids send the pressure to the correct clutch, on commands from the TCM.

The gearbox output turns the differential. The differential drives the two front drive shafts. On an AWD car the angle gear also turns the propeller shaft. The Haldex coupling at the rear axle closes when the front wheels turn faster than the rear wheels, and then the rear wheels push too.

Read the graph

  • Engine (amber) and turbine (white): a gap between them is slip in the torque converter. At a stop in D this gap is normal.
  • Wheels × ratio (cyan): the wheel speed, multiplied by the gear ratio. When a clutch holds, this line is on the turbine line. When a clutch slips, the turbine line goes up and the cyan line stays low.
  • If the engine goes to high rpm, but the turbine and the wheels stay at zero, the converter or the pump does not turn the gearbox.
  • If the engine stops at about 2300 rpm with full gas and the car stands still, something holds the car. The gearbox clutches hold. This is the stall speed.

Your symptom: it pushes about 10 cm, then stops

Two very different faults give this result. The tests in the Diagnose tab tell them apart.

  1. The forward clutch slips. The clutch holds for a moment when it fills with oil. Then the pressure goes down, because oil leaks out at a worn seal, or the friction plates are worn. The car rolls a short distance and stops. With gas, the engine revs up easily, but the car does not move. Reverse often still works, because reverse uses other clutches. Select Forward clutch slips and put the lever in D.
  2. Something holds the car. A seized brake, a parking brake that does not release, or a seized part of the drive line. The gearbox drives, but the car only pushes against the tyres and the mounts. That gives about 10 cm. When you select N, the car moves back. With gas, the engine is loaded and stops at about the stall speed. Select Car is held and put the lever in D.

The simple test: put the lever in N and let two persons push the car on level ground. A free car rolls easily. A held car does not roll.

Five tests you can do

  1. ATF level and colour. On the 5-speed the procedure uses a dipstick, the engine at idle and the ATF at the specified temperature. Use the procedure for your VIN. Red and clear is good. Dark with a burnt smell means worn clutches. Pink and milky means coolant in the ATF.
  2. Reverse. Put the lever in R at idle. If R is good and D is bad, the fault is in a forward element of the gearbox.
  3. Manual mode, gear 1. Move the lever to the side and push it down to 1. If gear 1 works in manual mode, but D does not move the car, the one-way clutch of 1st gear can be the cause. The model shows this for the 5-speed.
  4. Light gas in D. Hold the brake pedal. Give gas slowly to about 1500 rpm for 2 seconds, not more. Release. Do this only one time. If the engine revs up very easily and the car does not push, the clutch slips.
  5. Push test in N. See above.
Do not do a full stall test on a gearbox that slips. Full gas against a slipping clutch burns the plates in seconds. Chock the wheels. Keep persons away from the front and the rear of the car while it is in gear.

In VR (Meta Quest)

  • Open the page in the Quest browser and push Enter VR. The car is at its real size.
  • Point with a controller or your hand. Push the trigger or pinch to use a button or to select a part.
  • Left thumbstick: move. Right thumbstick left or right: turn in steps. Right thumbstick up or down: move up or down.
  • Use At the gearbox to stand next to the open engine bay, or On a lift to look up at the drive line.
  • Point at a panel and hold the grip button to move the panel. Release the grip to put it down.
  • Hold the two grip buttons, then move your hands apart to take the gearbox apart. Move them together to assemble it.
